This directory contains the following programmatically generated files:

- `arc_t3_bls12_381.bin`, the round constants for the ARC layer of Poseidon(T=3) over the BLS12-381
  scalar field
- `arc_t4_bls12_381.bin`, the round constants for the ARC layer of Poseidon(T=4) over the BLS12-381
  scalar field
- `arc_t3_bluesky.bin`, the round constants for the ARC layer of Poseidon(T=3) over the BlueSky
  scalar field
- `arc_t4_bluesky.bin`, the round constants for the ARC layer of Poseidon(T=4) over the BlueSky
  scalar field
- `g1.bin`, a KZG SRS containing 65536 $G_1$ points
- `g2.bin`, a KZG SRS containing 65536 $G_2$ points

`g1.bin` and `g2.bin` have been generated by the [`generate_params`][generate_params] script.

`arc_t3_*.bin` and `arc_t4_*.bin` have been generated by manually adapting the
[official Rust code][poseidon2-params] for BLS12-381 and BlueSky.

[generate_params]: https://github.com/libernet-mirror/generate_params
[poseidon2-params]: https://github.com/HorizenLabs/poseidon2/blob/main/plain_implementations/src/poseidon2/poseidon2_params.rs
